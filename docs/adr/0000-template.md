## Context

What is the problem we are trying to solve? What forces are at play?
What constraints exist? Cite earlier ADRs that apply.

Keep this factual. Do not yet describe the solution.

## Decision

State the decision in one or two sentences, then elaborate.

Use imperative language: "We use X.", "We do not use Y.", "Crate A
depends on crate B, never the other way around."

If multiple options were considered, list them briefly and explain why
the chosen option won. Do not pretend alternatives were never on the
table.

## Consequences

What follows from the decision?

- Positive consequences (what becomes easier).
- Negative consequences (what becomes harder, what costs we accept).
- Follow-up work (other ADRs to write, code to change, docs to update).

Be honest about the downsides. An ADR that lists only upsides is
suspicious.
